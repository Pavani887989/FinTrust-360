# AI Usage Documentation

## Project Name
FinTrust 360 – Financial Customer Complaints & Response Analytics

## AI Tool Used
ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## Purpose of AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used as a supporting tool during the development of this project.

The assistance included:

1. Understanding the project requirements and planning the workflow.
2. Getting guidance on Excel data cleaning and validation.
3. Understanding and preparing SQL queries for complaint analysis.
4. Developing and understanding DAX measures for Power BI KPIs.
5. Designing the Power BI dashboard and selecting suitable visualisations.
6. Interpreting analytical results and preparing business recommendations.
7. Organising project documentation and preparing the final report.

## Verification Process

- SQL queries were executed and their outputs were reviewed.
- Power BI measures and dashboard visualisations were checked.
- Calculated KPI values were compared with the available SQL results.
- Business findings and recommendations were reviewed before inclusion in the report.

## Declaration

AI was used as a supporting tool for learning, guidance and documentation. The project work, implementation, review and final decisions were carried out by the project author.
